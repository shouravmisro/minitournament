Frequently Asked Questions

What is ArenaPro and what sports does it support?

ArenaPro is a free web-based tournament, club, and matchday management platform built for football, 5-a-side/7-a-side turf leagues, cricket, esports, and local campus cups. It combines registration, live auction or wheel drafts, automated fixtures, standings with tiebreakers, knockout brackets, persistent clubs, and player career passports.

Is ArenaPro free for organizers, captains, and players?

Yes. Organizers can create unlimited tournaments, share 6-character join codes, run multi-device live player auctions, publish schedules, and track career stats for free without downloading an app.

How do live synced auction drafts and spin-the-wheel drafts work?

Captains sign in on their own devices using Google or a Captain PIN and bid live while the organizer controls nominations. ArenaPro automatically enforces squad budget guardrails and minimum reserve protection, or organizers can run an animated Spin-the-Wheel draft for balanced social leagues.

How do players join a tournament and claim existing stats?

Players enter a 6-character Join Code or click a direct invite URL to self-register into an available slot or automatic waitlist. Returning players who already appear in a legacy tournament can sign in with Google and link their existing profile so past goals, assists, and ratings merge into one verified career passport.

How are league tiebreakers and head-to-head results calculated?

Organizers choose custom tiebreaker priority rules—such as Goal Difference, Head-to-Head record, Goals Scored, or Wins—and ArenaPro updates the league table and Head-to-Head matrix instantly as final scores and shootout results are logged.
